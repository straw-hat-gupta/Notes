# GeoComply Graduate Rotational Program (Technology)

[Original job posting](https://jobs.lever.co/geocomply-2/4ea5981c-aadf-473e-bc26-f1ea3ec8af45)

*Markdown summary of the posting, paraphrased from GeoComply's description. Checked November 3, 2026.*

## At a glance

- **Location:** Vancouver, British Columbia; on site
- **Schedule:** Full time, 40 hours per week
- **Term:** 18 months, starting January 2027
- **Compensation:** CAD $60,000–$72,000 annually
- **Application deadline:** November 10, 2026, or when the posting reaches 250 applicants, whichever occurs first
- **Who can apply:** Current students and recent graduates from any discipline; the main description emphasizes graduates within two years of finishing school

## The opportunity

The program is intended for graduates who want to understand a technology company from product development through customer needs. Participants work across two or three functions over 18 months, taking responsibility for a concrete outcome in each rotation. GeoComply decides on the initial team and project after meeting candidates and exploring where their skills can make the greatest difference.

GeoComply develops technology that checks online users' identity and location. Its customers operate in areas including regulated gaming, prediction markets and financial technology.

## Available areas

**Technology:** software engineering (web, mobile and desktop); data and analytics; machine learning and fraud detection; product management; DevOps and cloud infrastructure; information security; AI and automation.

**Customer:** customer success; account management; solutions engineering; fraud and risk analysis; technical support and operations.

**Growth:** marketing; sales and business development; partnerships; revenue operations; government relations and policy.

**Operations:** people and culture; talent acquisition; learning and development; finance and accounting; legal and compliance; IT; incident management.

## Rotation structure

1. **Months 1–6, home rotation:** Join a team in your starting track, learn its products and customers, and deliver work that ships.
2. **Months 7–12, cross-track rotation:** Move between technical and customer-facing or commercial work to understand both product building and customer demand.
3. **Months 13–18, landing rotation:** Choose a final team with your manager and the Early Talent Program. This may be the team you join permanently afterward.

At the end of each rotation, participants present their work and its impact to company leaders.

## What you would do

- Own a deliverable from the initial conversations through implementation and use.
- Learn GeoComply's products, customers and regulated markets.
- Use AI, data and low-code tools while checking the quality of their outputs.
- Work with technical and commercial colleagues, learn from senior leaders and present results to executives.
- Find inefficient processes and improve them.
- Develop through mentorship, leadership training and the Early Talent Program.

## What GeoComply looks for

GeoComply wants someone who can make progress on an unclear problem, build a working solution, learn quickly and explain decisions clearly. The posting emphasizes practical AI use, comfort with messy data, resilience, collaboration and careful handling of confidential information. It describes its preferred mindset as passion, hunger and drive.

It also highlights five qualities: choosing growth over comfort; creating or fixing something without being asked; bringing an unusual strength; adapting when the brief is unclear; and actively seeking to understand how things work.

Experience in GeoComply's internships, a product that continued to be used after you built it, or exposure to identity, fraud, compliance or business-to-business software is a plus. The posting says candidates should still apply if they do not meet every listed criterion.

## Program support and outcomes

Each graduate has a manager, feedback and clear project goals, plus a buddy who previously completed the Early Talent Program. GeoComply describes opportunities to meet senior leaders, including a co-founder, early in the program. Projects are expected to produce results that matter to the business, and graduates share their impact with leadership. The posting says 8–10% of the company's workforce began in the Early Talent Program and describes a possible path into a permanent role after successful rotations.

## Hiring process

1. Apply with your preferred starting track and explain the contribution you could make.
2. Complete the online assessment built by a GeoComply intern.
3. Speak with the Talent team.
4. Complete a case study using fictional data, choosing a problem and showing what you build.
5. Meet a panel of leaders from both tracks. Candidates may also speak with several teams or managers before an offer for their first rotation and a tentative plan for later rotations.

The salary band reflects differences in graduates' education and prior work experience. GeoComply notes that AI tools may assist recruiters in reviewing application materials, while people make final hiring decisions.
