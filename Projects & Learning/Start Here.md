## How this folder works
- Existing notes outside this folder stay untouched; link to them as source material.
- Keep big outcomes in Big Tasks and break them into smaller steps in their own task or project folders when enough detail is available.
- Record unknowns rather than inventing priorities, deadlines, symptoms, or solutions.
- Link related notes with Obsidian links, including dependencies when known.
- Keep the main task list and affected detail notes consistent after each brain dump.
